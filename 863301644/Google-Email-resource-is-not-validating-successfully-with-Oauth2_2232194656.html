Magic Global Support : Google Email resource is not validating successfully with Oauth2

Created by Amjad Madre on 31,May, 2023

Email OAuth2 fails with an error "Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch ".

The customer cannot verify google E-Mail using OAuth2.

First, the customer was getting the message, that the "app" has not been validated.

When continuing the browser opens calling a URL that is not found.

To overcome this scenario:

Please change the lookback server settings (on the server) to http://localhost

OR

Change the Callback URL in the resource to http://localhost from https://localhost
