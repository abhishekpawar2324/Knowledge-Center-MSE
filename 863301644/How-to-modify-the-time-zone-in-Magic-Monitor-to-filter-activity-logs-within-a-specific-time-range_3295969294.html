Magic Global Support : How to modify the time zone in Magic Monitor to filter activity logs within a specific time range

Created by Shantanu Patil on 11,Mar, 2025

Description:
When trying to filter activity logs in Magic Monitor for a specific time range, the logs may not appear correctly due to a time zone mismatch. For example, if the system's time zone is set to Asia/Calcutta but the default time zone in the installation directory (C:\Magic xpi 4.14.1\InMemoryMiddleware\deploy\values.yaml) is configured as US/Central, the logs may not align with the desired time range.
