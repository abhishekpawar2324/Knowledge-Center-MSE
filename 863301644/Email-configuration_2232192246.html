Magic Global Support : Email configuration

Created by Amjad Madre on 31,May, 2023


Configuration of Email component resource for Yahoo and Gmail using App Password:
In the Magic XPI, the resource validation fails for the Gmail and yahoo account because of the password of the Google and yahoo account.
In other hands, if we put the login password of the account and try to validate the resource.
The resource will get validated successfully but in the runtime we will get the exception errors like
SMTPexception ..etc..
If we use 2-Step-Verification and are seeing a "password incorrect" error when trying to access your Google Account, an App password may solve the problem.
An App Password is a 16-digit passcode that gives a less secure app or device permission to access your Google Account. App Passwords can only be used with accounts that have 2-Step Verification turned on.
Steps for the generating the App password:

  1. Login into the Yahoo account.
  2. Go to the Account information
  3. Click Account Security -> Manage App password
  4. Click on the Select your app->choose 'Other app' option


  1. Enter the Name 'Magic XPI' and click on Generate tab.
  2. The password will generate.


  1. Copy this password into the password field of Email resource (Magic XPI) and validate the resource.
