Magic Global Support : Excel connector - special characters

Created by Amjad Madre on 31,May, 2023

To support German special characters (Ä,ä,Ü,ü) it is possible to change project encoding to “UTF-16“.

This might have an impact on other steps which use the project encoding definition and not a direct setting to a specific encoding.
