Magic xpi gets Memory shortage within GSC : Due to Local cache of Magic_Info .

When there are huge Activity logs are getting updated from the project, GSC receives Memory shortage due to Magic Info.

After adding "-Dcom.magicsoftware.xpi.info.localcach.size=0” in JVM_args in Magic.ini and Magicxpi-gs-agent.bat file
